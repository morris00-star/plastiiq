Cross-Functional Root Cause Analysis
The "Golden Thread" of Information

Many errors stem from failures in the information flow from Sales through to shipped product. The communication breakdown affects all departments and leads to costly errors and customer complaints.

Critical Information Flow
  • Sales must capture all customer requirements accurately
  • Production Planning must translate into precise work orders
  • Operators must follow SOPs and work orders exactly
  • Quality Control must verify at all critical control points
  • Logistics must ensure correct product delivery
Quality Management System
  • Implement robust QMS with clear procedures
  • Use checklists at all critical stages
  • Establish non-punitive error reporting culture
  • Regular cross-departmental meetings
  • Continuous improvement processes
Departmental Responsibilities
Sales

Accurate customer requirements, realistic promises

Production

SOP adherence, process control, quality focus

Quality

Verification, testing, continuous monitoring

Maintenance

Equipment reliability, preventive maintenance

Success Strategy

Implement a closed-loop communication system where feedback from each department informs process improvements. Regular cross-training and shared goals create a culture of quality and continuous improvement.
