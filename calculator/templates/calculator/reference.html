Why Use Laminates? (The "Curtain" Concept)
The act of lamination — combining materials — is necessary because no single plastic film offers all four required properties at once: strength, printability, high barrier, and heat sealability. Each layer in a laminate acts like a curtain contributing one property, and together they cover what no single film could deliver alone.

Material Selection Principles
  • Match material properties to product protection requirements
  • Balance quality needs with cost efficiency
  • Consider processing requirements (sealing, printing)
  • Evaluate end-use conditions (storage, transportation)
Lamination Benefits
  • Combines multiple properties in one structure
  • Enables high-barrier protection
  • Provides mechanical strength and durability
  • Allows optimal material usage and cost control
Professional Insight

Successful flexible packaging design requires understanding both material science and practical manufacturing constraints. The right material combination ensures product protection, consumer appeal, and production efficiency.

About Materials & Laminates
Understanding Material Selection

Each material and laminate is selected based on:

  • Barrier properties - O₂, moisture, light, aroma
  • Mechanical performance - Puncture, tensile, tear
  • Heat resistance - Retort, hot-fill, freeze
  • Sealability - Heat seal, cold seal, peelable
  • Aesthetics - Clarity, matte, gloss, pearlised
  • Cost-effectiveness - Material efficiency
