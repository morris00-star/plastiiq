Tolerances represent acceptable variation ranges in production. Tighter tolerances often mean higher costs but better consistency.

Dimensions & Physical Properties
Property Typical Tolerance / Range Unit Notes & Context
Thickness (Microns) ± 10% % Critical control parameter. ±5% is excellent for cast film; ±8-10% is common for blown film. Measured across the web and over time.
Width ± 5 mm mm Depends on slitter accuracy and web stability. Tighter tolerances (±1-2mm) are possible with precision slitting.
Yield ± 5% % Primary spec for converters. Directly calculated from thickness and density. Critical for cost calculations.
Density ± 0.5% to ± 1.5% g/cm³ Very tight control is possible as it's inherent to the resin. Affected by additives and mixing consistency.
Mechanical Properties
Property Typical Tolerance / Range Unit Notes & Context
Tensile Strength ± 10% to ± 20% % High variability due to test method and material orientation (MD/TD). Affected by polymer grade and processing conditions.
Elongation at Break ± 15% to ± 30% % Can be highly variable. Often has a minimum requirement rather than a tight symmetrical tolerance. Critical for flexible packaging applications.
Dart Impact Strength ± 15% to ± 25% % High inherent variability in the test method. Often reported as a minimum value for quality assurance.
Optical & Barrier Properties
Property Typical Tolerance / Range Unit Notes & Context
Haze ± 5% % e.g., If target is 6% haze, typical range is 4-8%. Tighter for optical-grade films. Critical for clarity applications.
MVTR
Moisture Vapor Transmission Rate		 ± 10% to ± 20% g/m²/day Barrier properties can vary with temperature, humidity, and film morphology. Critical for food packaging and moisture-sensitive products.
OTR
Oxygen Transmission Rate		 ± 10% to ± 20% cm³/m²/day Similar to MVTR, subject to test condition variability and material consistency. Essential for modified atmosphere packaging.

Processing & Lamination
Property Typical Tolerance / Range Unit Notes & Context
Viscosity (of coating) ± 10% cP or % Critical for coating weight control. Managed by solvent/adhesive formulation and process control.
Laminate Curing Time Minimum + 10% Time This is a process parameter, not a film property. Tolerance is usually a minimum requirement to ensure bond strength.
Treatment (Wetting Tension) Minimum 38 dynes/cm dynes/cm A minimum value is always specified to ensure printability/lamination. It decays over time. A typical initial value is 38-42 dynes/cm.

Key Takeaways & Practical Hints
Critical Control Points
  • Thickness tolerance directly impacts yield and material costs
  • Width control affects downstream processing efficiency
  • Yield calculation is essential for pricing and cost control
  • COF management crucial for packaging machine performance
Common Challenges
  • High variability in mechanical properties requires robust testing
  • Barrier properties affected by environmental conditions
  • Color consistency requires spectrophotometer monitoring
  • Waste reduction directly impacts profitability
Process Optimization Tips
For Thickness Control

Use automatic gauge control systems. Monitor profile consistency. Regular die maintenance.

For Mechanical Properties

Control orientation ratios. Maintain consistent cooling. Monitor resin lot variations.

For Color & Appearance

Standardize lighting conditions. Regular instrument calibration. Establish color standards.

Professional Insight

Successful quality control requires balancing tight tolerances with practical manufacturing constraints. Focus on critical-to-quality parameters while establishing realistic, achievable tolerance ranges that ensure product performance without unnecessary cost increases.
